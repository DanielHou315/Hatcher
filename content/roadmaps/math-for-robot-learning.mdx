## How to use this roadmap

Read one page a day. Each page is sized for a single sitting of thirty to sixty minutes and
ends with two to four exercises. Do the worked example with pen and paper before reading the
proof; the point of the site is to make the computations routine, not to admire them.

Stages are ordered so that no page appears before its prerequisites. Within a stage the items
are ordered by level. The three paper pages sit at the end of the stage that supplies their
mathematics, and each paper page's reading order section lists exactly which earlier pages it
draws on.

## What you should be able to do at the end

- Take an SDE from a paper, write its Fokker–Planck equation, reverse it in time, and derive
  the probability-flow ODE without looking anything up.
- Derive the flow matching objective from the continuity equation and explain why the
  conditional and marginal losses share a gradient.
- State the Schrödinger bridge problem as a KL projection, explain why Sinkhorn solves its
  static form, and follow the convergence argument for iterative proportional fitting.
- Read a KL-regularized RL objective and recognize the Boltzmann policy, the soft Bellman
  operator, and the control-as-inference derivation behind it.
- Handle rotations and poses as group elements and know what a gradient on SE(3) means.

## When a paper needs something that is not here

Use the plugin's paper scout and decomposer to pull the paper apart. Every prerequisite it
names that is missing from the site becomes a new concept page, and the roadmap curator places
it in the stage where its own prerequisites already live.
